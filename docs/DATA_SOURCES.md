# Data Sources, Licensing, and Provenance

This document specifies data sources, licensing terms, schemas, and offline caching strategies for RiskWise.

---

## 1. Credit Risk Benchmark Dataset

- **Dataset:** Indian Banking & CIBIL Bureau Merged Dataset
- **Repository / Provenance:** Merged from internal retail lending portfolio records and external CIBIL bureau trade-line history (`dataset/Internal_Bank_Dataset.xlsx` + `dataset/External_Cibil_Dataset.xlsx`) on unique borrower identifier `PROSPECTID`.
- **Records:** 51,336 retail loan borrower profiles.
- **Held-out Unseen Validation Set:** `dataset/Unseen_Dataset.xlsx` (100 candidate applicant records).
- **Target Variable:** `Approved_Flag` (Multiclass: P1 = Tier 1 Prime, P2 = Tier 2 Near-Prime, P3 = Tier 3 Moderate Risk, P4 = Tier 4 High Risk / Delinquent).
- **Features:** 42 core trade-line and inquiry variables:
  - 37 Numerical: Active and closed trade lines (`Total_TL`, `Tot_Closed_TL`, `Tot_Active_TL`), recent inquiries (`tot_enq`, `CC_enq_L12m`, `PL_enq_L12m`), delinquency metrics (`num_times_60p_dpd`, `max_recent_level_of_deliq`), income (`NETMONTHLYINCOME`), and employment tenure (`Time_With_Curr_Empr`).
  - 5 Categorical: `MARITALSTATUS`, `EDUCATION`, `GENDER`, `last_prod_enq2`, `first_prod_enq2`.
- **Target Leakage Prohibition:** `Credit_Score` (bureau cutoff score) is strictly excluded from production modeling to avoid circular target leakage (which produced artificial ROC-AUC 0.9999) and ensure generalizability to thin-file applicants.
- **Storage Location:**
  - Raw datasets: `dataset/External_Cibil_Dataset.xlsx`, `dataset/Internal_Bank_Dataset.xlsx`, `dataset/Unseen_Dataset.xlsx`
  - Cleaned / preprocessed: `data/processed/credit_indian_cleaned.csv`
- **Important Disclosure:** RiskWise credit tiers provide relative risk ranking and educational explainability. They do NOT represent a credit bureau score or automated statutory underwriting decision.

## 2. Market Historical Price Snapshots

- **Primary Source:** Daily adjusted close prices and historical trading volume.
- **Provider (Optional Refresh):** `yfinance` / Yahoo Finance public endpoints (subject to terms of service).
- **Committed Offline Strategy:** Local versioned CSV snapshots saved in `data/market_snapshots/`.
- **Supported Initial Universe:**
  - Standard Equity & Benchmark Tickers (e.g., `SPY`, `AAPL`, `MSFT`, `GOOGL`, or Indian indices/equities when currency is aligned).
  - Rule: Portfolios must be single-currency. Mixed-currency portfolio analysis is rejected unless currency conversion is explicitly configured.
- **Snapshot Manifest:** Saved in `data/manifests/market_manifest.json` detailing:
  - Ticker symbol
  - Start date & End date
  - Frequency (Daily)
  - Currency (USD / INR)
  - Price field used (Adjusted Close)
  - Retrieval timestamp
  - SHA-256 Checksum

## 3. Financial Education & RAG Corpus

- **Scope:** Curated, factual financial definitions, regulatory disclosures, and risk management concepts.
- **Sources:**
  - Public educational materials from regulatory frameworks (e.g., SEBI investor education, RBI financial education basics, Basel Committee definitions for VaR and backtesting).
- **Storage:** `knowledge/sources/` with manifest in `knowledge/manifest.csv`.
- **Integrity Rule:** No copyrighted textbooks or unauthorized web scraping. Citations generated by the Copilot must reference real entries in the manifest.

## 4. Knowledge Provenance Audit & Content Adjustments (Phase 1.5)

To guarantee academic and legal integrity:
- **Author Provenance Declaration:** All 6 documents under `knowledge/sources/` (`basel_irb_framework.md`, `var_backtesting_supervisory_framework.md`, `uci_credit_card_study.md`, `rbi_retail_loan_guidelines.md`, `sebi_investor_risk_metrics.md`, `personal_emergency_runway_standards.md`) are explicitly declared as **team-authored educational summaries**.

- **Manifest Columns:** `knowledge/manifest.csv` truthfully sets `provenance=team_summary`, `retrieved=false`, and leaves `retrieval_date` empty since materials were written locally and not retrieved via live scraper or API.

## 6. Drift Experiment Provenance & Partitioning Policy (Phase 2.1)

- **Labeling Standard:** Output generated by `scripts/run_drift_experiment.py` and the monitoring API is explicitly labeled as `"synthetic injected drift for pipeline validation"` across all script headers, API schemas (`drift_source`), and documentation.
- **Seeding:** All synthetic noise operations utilize fixed random seeds (`np.random.seed(42)` and `random_state=123`) to ensure complete reproducibility.
- **Partitioning Rule (Cross-Sectional Stratification Policy):**
  - In the Indian Banking & CIBIL Dataset, rows represent individual cross-sectional borrower accounts. Data is partitioned into stratified 70% train / 15% validation / 15% test splits using `Approved_Flag` strata with fixed random seeds (`random_state=42`).
  - Production drift monitoring computes Population Stability Index (PSI) against reference training baseline distributions across all 42 core trade-line and demographic features.
