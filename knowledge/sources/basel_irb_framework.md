# Basel II Framework: Internal Ratings-Based (IRB) Approach

> **Provenance Notice:** Team-authored educational summary based on Bank for International Settlements (BCBS) conceptual frameworks; not an official regulatory text.

## Credit Risk Measurement & Default Definition
Under the Basel II Framework (BCBS 128 publication concepts), institutions assessing borrower credit risk evaluate three core quantitative parameters:
1. Probability of Default (PD): The statistical likelihood that an individual borrower will default on a credit obligation over a defined horizon (typically one year).
2. Loss Given Default (LGD): The expected share of exposure lost in the event of default.
3. Exposure at Default (EAD): The gross monetary exposure outstanding at default.

## Qualitative Risk Rating Criteria
The IRB approach emphasizes that risk ratings and bands should reflect repayment capacity and debt obligations. Risk buckets (Low, Moderate, Elevated, High) provide operational risk grading. In academic proxy benchmarks such as credit-card delinquency datasets, default is frequently defined as payment delinquency exceeding 90 days.
